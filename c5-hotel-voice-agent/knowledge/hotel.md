# Clarium Bay Hotel

A made-up hotel for the Week 5 workshop. The agent answers policy questions only from this file.

## Check-in and check-out
Check-in is from 3:00 PM. Check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in from 12:00 PM is possible when a room is ready,
at no charge; we can't guarantee it in advance. Late check-out until 1:00 PM costs 30 dollars.

## Cancellation
Cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before the check-in date. After that, or if you don't arrive, we charge
the first night. To cancel, call the front desk with your confirmation code.

## Payment
We take all major credit cards. We hold the first night on your card when you check in, not when you book.
We don't take cash deposits.

## Breakfast
Breakfast is served in the Harbor Room from 6:30 to 10:30 AM on weekdays and 7:00 to 11:00 AM on weekends.
It costs 18 dollars per adult. Children under 12 eat free with an adult.

## Children and extra beds
Children of all ages are welcome. A crib is free on request. A rollaway bed costs 25 dollars a night and fits only
in a Deluxe King or Family Suite.

## Pets
Dogs up to 25 kilograms are welcome in Standard Queen rooms for 40 dollars per stay. Other pets aren't allowed.
Service animals are always welcome, at no charge.

## Wi-Fi and smoking
Wi-Fi is free throughout the hotel. All rooms and indoor spaces are non-smoking; a cleaning fee of 250 dollars
applies if someone smokes in a room.
